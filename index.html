v1.0 — Initial Launch


Welcome to the first release of the JB Hi-Fi Deals Filterer! This tool is designed to help you slice and dice through the JB Hi-Fi catalog to find hidden clearance gems and price drops.



Key Features



  • Instant Search: Type anything to filter thousands of products instantly. No page reloads.

  • Smart Categories: Filter by specific categories (Audio, Computers, Gaming, etc.) with one click.

  • Deep Discount Finder: Sort by % Off to find the biggest price drops first.

  • Mobile Optimized: A smooth, app-like experience on your phone without horizontal scrolling issues.

  • Dark Mode: Toggles automatically based on your system, or switch it manually to save your eyes at night.

  • Quick Links: Click the product title to go straight to the store page, or the G icon to Google it.
